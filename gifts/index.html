From neon-lit streets
to quiet mountain mornings.

We dream of discovering Japan together: wandering through Kyoto's peaceful lanes, tasting our way through Tokyo, watching the Shinkansen race across the countryside, and finding a little stillness in a traditional ryokan. If you would like to give us a wedding gift, you can be part of one of these unforgettable experiences.

Every contribution, large or small, helps make this journey our own.

A journey of contrasts

One country, a thousand memories.

We want to experience the many sides of Japan, from its lively cities and ancient temples to its beautiful landscapes and moments of calm.

01

Tokyo

Bright city lights, tiny ramen counters, hidden neighbourhoods and the thrill of getting happily lost together.

02

Kyoto

Wooden streets, lantern-lit evenings, temple gardens and early walks beneath the torii gates of Fushimi Inari.

03

Mount Fuji

A chance to slow down, admire the mountain from the lakeside and take in one of Japan's iconic views.

04

Hakone

A restorative stop for hot springs, mountain scenery and a night in a traditional Japanese inn.

05

By Shinkansen

Watching the landscape unfold from a train window, with a bento box in hand and nowhere else to be.

06

Little discoveries

Tea ceremonies, local markets, seasonal sweets and the unexpected moments that become our favourite stories.
